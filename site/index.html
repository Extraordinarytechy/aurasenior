AuraSenior

A voice companion on WhatsApp for older parents, with updates their family can act on.

How it works

  • Each morning it checks in with a WhatsApp voice note in the parent's language (Hindi or English).
  • The parent replies by voice. It notes medicines taken or finished and remembers what matters to them.
  • When a medicine runs out, a family member gets one message with an "Order refill" button. One tap sends the order email to the pharmacy.
  • If a check-in gets no reply, or the parent says they fell or need help, family is told at once.
  • Every Sunday, family and the doctor get a short email of what the parent reported that week.

What it is not

AuraSenior is not a medical device and not an emergency service. It never diagnoses, names an illness, gives a health score or advises on doses. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Status

This is a preview service running in a test setup. Messages go only to people who have agreed to receive them. Anyone can stop at any time by replying STOP.
