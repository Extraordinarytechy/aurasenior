Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026. AuraSenior is a preview service run in a test setup.

Who we message

We only message people a family administrator has added to a household and who have agreed to receive messages: by replying YES on WhatsApp or RCS, or by agreeing to email updates. Anyone can reply STOP at any time, and we stop messaging them on that channel.

What we keep

  • Names, phone numbers, email addresses, language and preferred channel of household members.
  • Messages and voice notes sent to AuraSenior, and their transcripts.
  • What the parent reports: medicines taken or finished, refill approvals, check-in replies, and requests for help.
  • Short notes the assistant keeps to remember the parent's routine and the people they mention.
  • Delivery status of messages we send.

How we use it

Only to run the service for that household: to reply to the parent, to tell family about refills, missed check-ins and requests for help, to send the pharmacy a refill order the family approved, and to send the weekly summary. We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored and who processes it

Data is stored in Amazon Web Services in the US East (N. Virginia) region. Messages are carried by WhatsApp (Meta), RCS and SMS through AWS End User Messaging, and email through Amazon SES. Replies are written by an AI model on Amazon Bedrock; voice notes are transcribed by Amazon Transcribe and replies are spoken by Amazon Polly.

How long we keep it

Assistant memory events expire after 30 days. Household records are kept while the household uses the service and deleted on request.

Health information

AuraSenior is not a medical device. It records what the parent says about medicines and wellbeing so their family can stay informed. It does not diagnose or give medical advice.

Your choices

Reply STOP to stop messages on a channel. To see or delete your data, write to the contact address below.
